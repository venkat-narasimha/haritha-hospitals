Company

A Company is the legal entity that owns every transaction in ERPNext. Every Employee, Department, and Branch links back to a Company.

Company is the top of the hierarchy. All transactional data — payroll, invoices, expenses — is scoped to a Company. In multi-company setups, each Company is fully isolated.

Transition: "Inside a Company, work is grouped by Department."
7 / 17

Department

A Department is a functional unit within a Company — Nursing, Finance, IT. Departments group employees for reporting and approval routing.

  • Tree structure (parent + child departments).
  • Approval authority per department.
  • Cost-center linkage.
Departments form a tree, not a flat list. Useful for org charts, approval flows, and cost-center rollups. Define reporting line cleanly here.

Transition: "Departments describe function — Designations describe role."
8 / 17

Designation

A Designation is the job title or role an Employee holds — Staff Nurse, Surgeon, HR Executive. It defines position-level expectations and salary bands.

  • Links to Employee Grade for compensation.
  • Drives approval limits.
  • Appears on payslips and reports.
Staff Nurse
Grade: T2
Senior Surgeon
Grade: T6
Operations Manager
Grade: T5
Designations are roles, not people. The same designation can be held by many employees. Keep the list tight — titles should reflect real job families, not individual career tracks.

Transition: "Work happens in a place — Branch."
9 / 17

Branch

A Branch is a physical or logical location where work happens — a hospital site, a regional office, a warehouse. Employees are assigned to one Branch for attendance and headcount reporting.

  • Enables multi-site payroll.
  • GPS-tagged check-in scope.
  • Per-branch holiday list override.
Branch is the operational location. It separates Company (legal) from place (operational). The 200m check-in radius is illustrative — it is configurable per Branch.

Transition: "Compensation levels live in Grade."

Grade

A Grade is a pay band or seniority tier — T1, T2, T3, T4. It links a Designation to a compensation range and approval limit.

  • Drives payroll structure.
  • Default leave policy per grade.
  • Approval authority thresholds.

Grades are the bridge between role (Designation) and money (Payroll). Keep grades few — 4 to 8 — so compensation review cycles stay clean.

Employment Type

An Employment Type defines the contractual engagement — Full-time, Part-time, Contract, Intern. It governs which policies apply.

  • Leave accrual rules per type.
  • Probation period per type.
  • Contract end-date tracking.
Full-time

fixed end-date

6-month engagement
Employment Type is the engagement lens. The same Designation (e.g., Engineer) can be Full-time or Contract with very different policies. Keep types few and mutually exclusive.

Employee Master: Key Fields

The Employee DocType is the heart of HRMS. It links one person to a Company, Department, Branch, Designation, Grade, and Employment Type — and captures the field groups below.

Field Group Key Fields Purpose
Personal name, date_of_birth, contact Identification, emergency contact, statutory age checks.
Joining date_of_joining, confirmation_date, probation Tenure tracking, probation completion, anniversary triggers.
Employment employment_type, grade, branch, department Day-to-day reporting structure and policy scope.
Approvers leave_approver, expense_approver, shift_request_approver Auto-populated on request documents; routed for approval.
Statutory PAN, IFSC, PF_account_number Payroll, bank disbursal, statutory filings.
Exit relieving_date, exit_status, final_settlement
Employee is the only required master — the six ring entities are optional references. Walk the audience through each row of the table: personal identity, joining tenure, employment structure, who approves what, statutory identifiers for payroll, and the offboarding trail.

Transition: "Now the relationships between these entities."

Hierarchy & Relationships

  • Company → Department (one-to-many).
  • Employee → Branch (operational assignment).
  • Employee → Designation + Grade + Employment Type (linked dimensions).
Stress that hierarchy is enforced by links, not by inheritance. An Employee can move between Departments; the link updates and history is preserved.
