## Training delivery — sign-off checklist

- [ ] **Training plan** delivered covering all roles: HR Lead (4h), HR Executive (8h), Department Manager (4h), Shift Supervisor (4h), Employee self-service (1h), IT Admin (4h)
- [ ] **Training sessions completed** with attendance recorded (per role) — minimum 90% of identified user base attended
- [ ] **Training materials** handed over to client (slide decks, runbooks, recorded videos, user guides)
- [ ] **User guides** (per role) published to a shared location accessible to all trainees
- [ ] **Recorded demo videos** produced for self-service onboarding of new joiners post-go-live
- [ ] **Train-the-trainer session** completed — at least 2 client-side trainers certified to run future trainings
- [ ] **Q&A log** captured with answers; unresolved items added to risk register
- [ ] **Hands-on lab exercise** completed by each trainee (or trainer-attested proxy for offline users)
- [ ] **Feedback survey** collected from each session (rated ≥3/5 average; any <2 escalated)
- [ ] **User Acceptance of Training** — trainees confirm they can perform their day-1 tasks independently
