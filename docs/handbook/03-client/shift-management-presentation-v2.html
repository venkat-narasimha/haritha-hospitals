Shift Type

A Shift Type is a reusable template that defines when work happens. You define Morning, Evening, and Night once, then assign employees to instances of these templates on specific dates.

Shift Types are reusable structural templates, not calendar dates. You configure timing rules once and leverage them endlessly. For example, clinical wards standardise around 8-hour rotations.

Transition: But work happens at a place — that's Shift Location.
7 / 18

Shift Location

  • Defines the physical deployment boundary tied directly to a scheduled shift.
  • Prevents fraudulent check-ins and buddy-punching via geo-fenced boundaries.
  • Configured via GPS coordinates paired with strict allowed radial tolerances.
Shift Locations prevent off-site time fraud by pairing mobile or kiosk check-ins with precise device telemetry. This boundary ensures staff check in on site.

Transition: Templates are scheduled — Shift Schedule.
8 / 18

Shift Schedule

  • Represents planned working patterns projected across a specified date calendar range.
  • Supports structured recurrences: weekly cycles, rolling rotations, or fixed templates.
  • Acts as the authoritative master template for generating actual employee assignments.
Keep clear separation between schedules and assignments: schedules provide the abstract planning pattern, whereas assignments represent the concrete operational reality.
