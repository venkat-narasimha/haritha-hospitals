Why lifecycle management matters

  • Manual checklists get lost; structured handovers reduce errors.
  • Compliance at join + exit is mandatory (HIPAA training, relieving date, exit clearance).
  • Growth tracking requires documented state changes (promotions, transfers, role changes).
Frame the cost: manual onboarding checklists get lost (gaps in training records); exit paperwork is incomplete (legal exposure); promotions are informal (grievances). Structured lifecycle fixes all three.

Employee Onboarding (entry path)

Employee Onboarding is a structured checklist that brings a new hire on board. It assigns tasks to specific users with due dates; completion triggers automatic Employee creation when paired with onboarding programs.

  • Task lists per role (doctor / nurse / technician / admin).
  • Due dates + assignees per task.
  • Completion audit trail for compliance.

Onboarding Tasks & Templates

An Onboarding Template is a reusable bundle of tasks (e.g., "Issue ID badge", "Complete HIPAA training", "Orient to ward 3") that HR can attach to a new Onboarding record.

  • Per-role templates (doctor, nurse, technician, admin).
  • Task dependencies (e.g., "Issue ID" before "Grant badge access").
  • Progress tracking per task.

Employee Promotion (role/grade/date change)

Employee Promotion is a formal record of a role or grade change. It updates the Employee's designation + grade + effective date, often triggering a Salary Structure Assignment update.

  • promotion_date — when the change takes effect.
  • new_designation + new_grade — the target state.
  • Approval workflow per HR policy.

Employee Transfer (intra-company move)

Employee Transfer moves an Employee between Departments or Branches within the same Company. It updates the Employee's department + branch + effective date.

  • Intra-company only — separate from Promotion.
  • new_department + new_branch — destination.
  • Approval workflow per HR policy.

Employee Separation (exit path)

Employee Separation is the exit process. Approval flips Employee.status to "Left" and sets a mandatory Relieving Date. Triggers Leave Encashment calculation and final settlement processing.

  • Status flip → Left.
  • Relieving Date mandatory.
  • Leave Encashment + final settlement hooks.

Employee Skill Map lets HR record skills per employee with proficiency levels. Useful for capability planning, training gap analysis, and roster assignment by skill.
