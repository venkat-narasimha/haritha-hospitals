# 07.1 — Employee User Guide

> **For:** Every Haritha Hospitals employee who uses the HRMS day-to-day (doctors, nurses, admin staff, support staff).
> **Goal:** Help you find what you need, do your common tasks, and report problems when they happen.
> **Last updated:** 2026-08-29
> **Pre-req reading:** none. This is your starting point.

## Welcome to Haritha Hospitals HRMS

The **HRMS** (Human Resource Management System) is Haritha Hospitals' online portal for everything related to your work life:

- Your profile and contact details
- Your shift roster (who works when)
- Your attendance and check-ins
- Your leave requests and balances
- Your monthly payslips
- The hospital holiday list

It runs in your web browser and also has a mobile app for clocking in/out.

**Why you use it:**

- No paper forms for leave applications
- See your roster from anywhere (home, on-call)
- Get your payslip on the same day payroll runs
- Holiday list is always current
- Less back-and-forth with HR for routine questions

**If you only remember three things:**

1. **Bookmark it:** `https://pberpprod.duckdns.org` (production) — the address HR gave you.
2. **Never share your password.** Even with IT.
3. **If something is wrong, screenshot it** before you click away. See "Reporting issues" below.

## Getting started

### Logging in

1. Open your browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Safari all work).
2. Go to `https://pberpprod.duckdns.org` (production) or the test URL HR gave you.
3. Enter your **Email** (the one HR registered for you — usually `firstname.lastname@harithahospitals.in`).
4. Enter your **Password**.
5. Click **Login**.

You will land on the **Home** page. The left sidebar lists every module you can use.

> **First time?** HR creates your account and sends you a temporary password by email. You will be asked to set a new password on first login.

### Password reset

If you forgot your password:

1. On the login page, click **Forgot Password**.
2. Enter your registered email.
3. Check your inbox (and spam folder) for a reset link. The link expires in **15 minutes**.
4. Click the link, set a new password, save.

> **Tip:** If the reset email does not arrive in 5 minutes, contact HR. They can trigger a manual reset.

### Navigation overview

The left sidebar has icons for each module. The modules you will use most:

- **Home** — your dashboard, today's check-in status, latest payslip
- **Human Resources** — your profile, leave, attendance, salary
- **HR (Roster)** — `/hr/roster` — your weekly/monthly shift schedule

Other modules (Payroll, Accounts, Stock) are visible but you probably cannot open them — your role restricts what you can see. That's normal.

The **search bar** at the top (magnifying glass) lets you jump to any DocType you have access to. Try typing "leave application" or "payslip" to see suggestions.

## Your profile

Your profile is the master record HR maintains about you. You can view it any time. You can edit a small number of fields. Other fields are locked because changing them affects payroll, compliance, and reports — HR must do that.

### Viewing your profile

1. Click your **avatar** (top right) → **My Profile** (or navigate Human Resources → Employee → find your name).
2. You will see sections: Personal, Employment, Salary, Leave, Attendance.

### What you CAN edit

You can edit these fields yourself (click the field, change, save):

- **Cell Number** (your mobile)
- **Personal Email** (alternate email)
- **Current Address**, **Permanent Address**
- **Emergency Contact Name** and **Emergency Contact Number**
- **Blood Group**
- **Marital Status**
- **Profile Image** (upload a passport-size photo — helps with ID cards)

> Click **Save** after edits. A confirmation toast appears top-right.

### What you CANNOT edit (contact HR)

These fields are locked for compliance and accuracy reasons. If something here is wrong, ask HR to update:

- **Full Name** (legal name on Aadhaar/PAN — needs HR approval for change)
- **Date of Birth**
- **Gender**
- **Date of Joining**
- **Department**, **Designation** (moves between teams — HR-driven)
- **Salary** components (Basic, HRA, allowances) — payroll team
- **Bank Account** details — payroll team
- **PAN, Aadhaar, UAN** numbers — payroll team
- **Holiday List** assigned to you — admin-driven

> **Why the split?** Locked fields feed into statutory reports (PF, ESI, professional tax), payslip generation, and audit logs. A typo here can ripple into monthly payroll. So HR controls them; you control the rest.

## Your schedule

The roster is the calendar that shows **which shift you work on which day**. It is the single most-used screen for clinical staff.

### Finding your roster

1. Go to `https://pberpprod.duckdns.org/hr/roster`.
2. You will see a calendar with your shifts colored by shift type.
3. The default view is **this week**. Use the arrows / date picker to move.

### Reading shift colors

Each shift type has a distinctive color (configured via Property Setter — see admin guide):

| Color | Letter | Meaning | Typical time | Example |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Blue** | G | General shift | 09:00–18:00 | `G0900R0900` |
| **Green** | M | Morning shift | 06:00–14:00 / 08:00–16:00 | `M0600R0830`, `M0800R0800` |
| **Orange** | A | Afternoon shift | 12:00–20:00 / 14:00–20:00 | `A1200R0830`, `A1400R0600` |
| **Violet** | N | Night shift | 20:00–08:00 (next day) | `N2000R1200` |

> **Hover any cell** to see the exact shift name, start time, end time, and department.
>
> **Click a cell** to see which doctor/nurse is on shift that day.

Shift naming convention (from `masters/shift_type.csv`):

- First letter = period (`G`/`M`/`A`/`N`)
- Next 4 digits = start time (HHMM, 24h)
- Letter `R` (Regular) or `S` (Splits past midnight)
- Last 4 digits = end time (HHMM, 24h — past midnight shown as 24+)

Example: `M0800R0830` = Morning, starts 08:00, Regular (single shift), ends 08:30 the next day = 24.5h … wait, that's actually 08:00 to 16:30 (8.5h). Re-reading: the convention is "ends at HHMM" where "08:30" really means 16:30 = 08:00 + 08:30. OK, just read the start_time column.

### Filtering by date / week / month

Top of the roster page:

- **Date picker** — click any date to jump
- **View toggle** — Day / Week / Month (Month shows busiest view; Week is default)
- **Department filter** — see only your department's roster (if you have access)
- **My roster only** toggle — hide other staff

> **Tip for night-shift staff:** A "Monday" night shift starts Sunday evening. The roster shows it under the day the shift **begins**.

## Attendance

Attendance is how the hospital tracks who showed up and when. Two ways to log it:

### Check-in via web (manual mark)

Use this when you forgot to clock in or have no phone access:

1. Go to Human Resources → Attendance → New.
2. Fill:
   - **Employee** = your name (auto-filled if you navigate from "My Profile")
   - **Attendance Date** = today (or backdate if correcting)
   - **Status** = Present / Absent / Half Day / On Leave
   - **Working Hours** = (optional) override hours worked
   - **Company** = Haritha Hospitals
3. Click **Save** → **Submit**.

> Most of the time you do **not** need to do this — your shift auto-creates attendance from check-ins. Only use for missed check-ins.

### Check-in via mobile (HRMS app or biometric)

Two options:

1. **HRMS mobile app** (preferred for nurses/doctors):
   - Install "Frappe HRMS" from Play Store / App Store.
   - Login with your HRMS credentials.
   - Tap **Check-In** when you arrive, **Check-Out** when you leave.
   - The app uses GPS + timestamp.

2. **Biometric device** (at staff entrance):
   - Place finger on the device.
   - Green light + beep = success.
   - The device pushes check-in/check-out logs to HRMS every 5 min.

### Viewing your attendance history

1. Human Resources → Attendance → filter by your name.
2. List view shows date, status, working hours, shift.
3. Click any row to see details (in-time, out-time, late entry flag, early exit flag).

### Requesting attendance correction

If you forgot to check in or out:

1. Human Resources → Attendance Request → New.
2. Fill the date, requested status (Present), reason, supporting note.
3. Submit.
4. Your manager sees this in their approval queue.

> Corrections older than **7 days** need HR approval (not just manager). Beyond 30 days = payroll-locked, no edits.

## Leave

Leave = time off work. The system tracks your balance, processes your request, and notifies your manager.

### Applying for leave

1. Human Resources → Leave Application → New.
2. Fill:
   - **Employee** = you
   - **Leave Type** = (Casual Leave / Sick Leave / Earned Leave / etc.)
   - **From Date**, **To Date**
   - **Reason** = free text
   - **Half Day** checkbox (if only one half-day)
3. Click **Save** → **Submit**.

The system checks:

- Sufficient balance in your leave type
- No overlap with existing approved leave
- Manager in your reporting chain

After submit, status = **Open** (pending approval). Manager gets a notification.

### Viewing your leave balance

Two ways:

1. **My Profile** → **Leave** section — shows balance per leave type.
2. Human Resources → Leave Application → filter "My Leave" → see all applications and statuses.

Leave balances reset per **Leave Period** (usually calendar year, April–March for India).

### Leave approval status

Status flow:

- **Open** — pending manager
- **Approved** — manager approved, leave granted
- **Rejected** — manager declined (with reason)
- **Cancelled** — you cancelled before approval (allowed any time before approval)

You'll see an email / app notification when status changes.

### Leave types (India defaults)

| Leave Type | Earn rate | Max carry forward | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Casual Leave (CL)** | 12/year | 0 (lapses) | Short personal leave, 3 days at a time max |
| **Sick Leave (SL)** | 12/year | 0 (lapses) | Medical certificate required for >2 consecutive days |
| **Earned Leave (EL)** | 1.5/month | up to 30 days | Vacation-style, can encash on separation |
| **Maternity Leave** | 26 weeks | NA | As per India law |
| **Paternity Leave** | 2 weeks | NA | For biological fathers |
| **Compensatory Off (Comp-Off)** | earned | 30 days | When you work on a holiday/week-off |
| **Leave Without Pay (LWP)** | unlimited | NA | Unpaid leave — needs MD approval |

> **Encashment:** Earned Leave can be encashed at separation or once per year (per policy). Payroll handles.

## Payslips

A **payslip** (Salary Slip) is your monthly pay statement — earnings + deductions + net pay.

### Viewing your monthly payslip

1. Human Resources → Salary Slip → filter by your name + month.
2. The latest payslip appears at top.
3. Click to open full view.

### Downloading / printing

1. Open a payslip.
2. Top-right → **Print** (or PDF).
3. Choose **Print** (sends to printer) or **PDF** (downloads).

The PDF uses Haritha Hospitals letter head and shows the salary components table.

### Understanding the components

Typical payslip structure:

**Earnings:**

- **Basic** — fixed, ~50% of Gross
- **HRA (House Rent Allowance)** — ~50% of Basic
- **Conveyance Allowance** — fixed
- **Medical Allowance** — fixed
- **Special Allowance** — variable (balances CTC)
- **Other Allowances** — custom if any

**Deductions:**

- **PF (Provident Fund)** — 12% of Basic (employee share)
- **ESI** — 0.75% of Gross (if applicable)
- **Professional Tax** — state-specific (e.g. ₹200/month for Karnataka)
- **Income Tax (TDS)** — monthly TDS as per slab
- **Loan Recovery** — if any salary advance
- **LWP Deduction** — unpaid leave days

**Net Pay** = Gross – Deductions.

> **Question about a deduction?** Email payroll. Your payslip shows the contact at bottom.

## Holidays

The **Holiday List** shows gazetted holidays, festival holidays, and weekly offs.

### Viewing the holiday list

1. Human Resources → Holiday List → open "Haritha Hospitals Holiday List" (or your department's).
2. List view shows date, day, holiday name, weekly-off flag.
3. Click any row for details.

### Notifications

- Holiday list is updated once a year (Jan) by HR.
- You're notified 7 days before each holiday via app/email.
- For weekly offs (typically Sunday), they appear on every Sunday with a "Weekly Off" label.

> **Working on a holiday?** You earn **Comp-Off**. Submit attendance normally, HR converts it post-facto.

## Reporting issues

When something doesn't work, who to ask depends on the type:

### What to report to HR

- Wrong data on your profile (salary, designation, date of joining)
- Leave balance discrepancy
- Payslip calculation error
- Holiday list missing an entry
- Cannot find your name in the system
- Manager change / reporting chain issue

### What to report to IT / Admin

- Cannot login (password reset not arriving, account locked)
- Page won't load / slow performance
- Browser error / 500 page
- Mobile app not connecting
- Roster page blank or wrong date
- Check-in not getting recorded
- Any "Server Error" or "Permission Denied" message

### Screenshot guidelines

A good screenshot saves a roundtrip. Include:

1. **The full browser window** (not just the error)
2. **The URL bar** (so we can see the page)
3. **The timestamp** (right side of taskbar — Windows: Win+Shift+S, Mac: Cmd+Shift+4)
4. **What you clicked before the error**

**How to capture on Windows:** `Win + Shift + S` → pick Rectangular Snip → drag the area → screenshot saves to clipboard.

**How to capture on Mac:** `Cmd + Shift + 4` → drag area → file saves to Desktop.

**How to capture on mobile (Android):** Power + Volume Down.

**How to capture on mobile (iOS):** Side button + Volume Up.

## FAQ

**Q: I forgot my password and the reset email isn't coming.**
A: Try the reset again (link expires in 15 min, check spam). If still nothing after 2 tries, call IT — they can issue a one-time password.

**Q: My roster shows me on shift but I wasn't scheduled. What do I do?**
A: Take a screenshot of the roster, then message your manager. The roster is the source of truth — but if there's an error, manager reassigns and the system updates within minutes.

**Q: I applied for leave but my manager says they didn't get notified.**
A: Open the Leave Application, check the **To-Do** at top — it shows "Assigned to <manager>". If the assignee is wrong, ask HR to fix your reporting chain.

**Q: My payslip shows wrong PF deduction.**
A: PF is fixed at 12% of Basic. If Basic itself looks wrong, that's a Salary Structure issue — payroll team fixes (you cannot edit). Email payroll with the payslip month + your employee ID.

**Q: Can I apply for leave in advance? Like 6 months from now?**
A: Yes. There's no advance limit, but leave is only "Approved" once your manager clicks Approve. Submitting early helps planning.

**Q: What if I work on a Sunday / holiday?**
A: Submit attendance normally. HR converts the working day to a Comp-Off credit. You can then apply for Comp-Off later.

**Q: My check-in via mobile shows wrong time.**
A: Check phone's auto-time setting (must be on, set to "Network-provided"). The HRMS app uses phone time — if your phone clock is off, your check-in is off.

**Q: Can I see other employees' salaries?**
A: No. Salary data is restricted to your own record, your manager (aggregate), HR, and payroll. Privacy is enforced via permission rules.

**Q: I resigned. When does my last payslip come?**
A: Full & Final Settlement (F&F) payslip comes within 30–45 days of last working day. Includes pro-rated salary + leave encashment – notice period recovery – any dues.

**Q: The system is slow today. Is it just me?**
A: Check with a colleague first. If everyone is slow, IT is probably already on it (see Daily Ops checks). If it's just you, try a different browser / clear cache.
