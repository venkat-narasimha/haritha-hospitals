Why leave management matters

  • Manual registers create disputes — accruals drift, balances are wrong, payouts get contested.
  • Multiple Leave Types per policy (sick, casual, earned, comp-off) need rules, not judgement calls.
  • Audit trail mandatory for compliance — every movement must trace to a source document.
  • Accrual, carry-forward, and encashment add arithmetic that paper registers cannot reliably do.
Frame the pain: HR teams track leave in Excel registers, accrue balances on paper, and resolve disputes on a case-by-case basis. A leave module centralizes allocation rules, automates balance calculations, and posts an immutable ledger. The chart shows dispute volume dropping once the system is live.

Holiday List

A Holiday List is the calendar of organizational + statutory holidays for a Company. It's the foundation for leave date calculations and Attendance 'On Leave' auto-creation.

  • Combines org-level + statutory holidays in one ordered list.
  • Date range via from_date and to_date.
  • Referenced by Attendance and the Leave Module when computing working-day counts.
Holiday Lists define which dates are non-working. A Company can have multiple lists (e.g., one per region). They are referenced by Attendance and the Leave module to compute working-day counts — so a 5-day leave spanning a holiday becomes 4 working days, not 5.

Leave Type

A Leave Type defines a kind of leave — Sick, Casual, Privilege, Earned, Compensatory — with its own rules: is_paid, carry_forward, encashable, max_continuous_days.

  • Rules-based config per type, applied automatically to every allocation.
  • Earned (annual accrual) vs non-earned (sick, casual) categories.
  • max_days_allowed caps total balance per employee per period.

Leave Period

A Leave Period bounds the annual leave cycle — from_date to to_date. Allocations and applications are scoped to a period.

  • from_date — first day of the cycle.
  • to_date — last day of the cycle.
  • Period name (e.g., "FY 2025–2026") for human-readable reference.
Most companies run a Jan-Dec cycle; some run fiscal Apr-Mar. Periods enable period-over-period carry-forward and clean balance resets at year-end. One Company can run parallel periods (e.g., transition quarters during a cycle change).

Leave Policy

A Leave Policy bundles multiple Leave Types with allocation rules. One Leave Policy per role-group is a common pattern — e.g., "Staff Nurse Policy" = 12 CL + 6 SL + 24 EL.

  • Policy name (e.g., "Staff Nurse Policy").
  • Included Leave Types with allocation per type.
  • Applied via Leave Policy Assignment (next slide).

Leave Policy Assignment

A Leave Policy Assignment binds a specific Leave Policy + Leave Period to a specific Employee. One Assignment per (employee, period) pair typically.

  • Employee + Policy + Period — the three-field binding.
  • effective_date — when the assignment starts (supports backdating).
  • Optional role/grade filter to enforce who gets what policy.

The Assignment is what triggers allocation. New joiners get their first assignment on confirmation; existing employees get one per cycle. Backdating is supported for corrections — e.g., a promotion that retroactively moves an employee to a higher-leave policy.

Leave Allocation

A Leave Allocation is the granted count of leaves for an Employee per Leave Type per Leave Period. Auto-generated when Leave Policy Assignment is created.

  • One row per (employee, leave_type, leave_period) triple.
  • total_leaves_allocated — the granted count.
  • Supports carry-forward from prior period (where Leave Type allows).
Allocations are the per-type balances. They are auto-created from policy assignment but can be adjusted manually — e.g., a one-time bonus allocation. Carry-forward from the prior period is supported where the Leave Type allows it, and is recorded as a fresh allocation row.

Leave Approval Workflow

When an employee submits a Leave Application, it routes to Employee.leave_approver. Approval flips status to "Approved" and posts to the Leave Ledger Entry.

Approval is a single-user flow routed to Employee.leave_approver. On approval, the Leave Application flips to "Approved" and posts a Leave Ledger Entry; on rejection, the employee is notified with a reason.

Approval is a single-user flow. The Employee.leave_approver field is the routing target — typically the reporting manager or department head. Approval flips status to "Approved" and posts a Leave Ledger Entry that decrements the allocation. Rejection notifies the employee with a reason and leaves the allocation untouched.

Leave Application

A Leave Application is the per-request record. It captures employee + leave_type + from_date + to_date + reason + half-day flag + supporting attachment.

  • Per-request lifecycle: Open → Approved / Rejected / Cancelled.
  • Half-day support (first half / second half).
  • Attachment field for medical certificates, exam call letters.

Compensatory Leave Request

A Compensatory Leave Request converts extra hours worked — overtime, holiday duty, on-call coverage — into leave credit. Approval posts to Leave Allocation as additional balance.

  • Sources: overtime, holiday duty, manual grant.
  • Standard approver workflow applies.
  • On approval: balance is added to the employee's Comp-off allocation.

Comp-off is one of the most-misconfigured Leave Types. Rules: only valid for extra hours worked, must be approved, posts as additional allocation, typically has an expiry (e.g., 90 days from grant). Track sources carefully — mixing holiday duty with overtime into one comp-off pool causes disputes.

Leave Encashment

Leave Encashment converts unused leave balance to cash at exit (or optionally during employment, per Leave Type rules). Pairs with Employee Separation.

  • Captures encashment_date, leave_type, and amount.
  • Payout via Salary Slip or Additional Salary.
  • Tied to Leave Ledger Entry for audit traceability.

Encashment closes the loop on unused leave. Encashable types — typically Earned/Privilege — compute a daily rate from gross salary. Encashment posts a final Leave Ledger Entry that zeroes the unused balance and triggers payroll. Disabled for non-encashable types like Sick Leave.

Leave Block List

A Leave Block List prevents leave applications on specified dates — e.g., audit week, statutory event, busy season. Overlaps with Holiday List for full coverage.

  • block_dates field captures date ranges.
  • Applies to all or specific Leave Types.
  • Complements Holiday List — holidays say "non-working"; blocks say "no leave granted".
Block Lists complement Holiday Lists. Holidays say "non-working"; Block Lists say "no leave granted". Common during financial year-end close, audit weeks, and statutory events that demand full attendance. Per-type filtering lets you block Casual but allow Sick.

Leave Ledger Entry

A Leave Ledger Entry is an append-only audit record of every leave movement — allocation, application, encashment. Source-of-truth for balance calculation.

  • Append-only — no edits, no deletes.
  • One row per (employee, leave_type, transaction).
  • transaction_type: Allocation / Application / Encashment.

The Ledger is append-only — no edits, no deletes. Balances are derived by summing entries. This makes compliance audits simple: every balance line is traceable to a source document (Leave Application, Leave Policy Assignment, or Encashment). The reference column points to the originating record.

Conclusion + Next Steps

Key Takeaways

  1. Leave is policy-driven and audit-tracked — not just a register of dates.
  2. 3-layer architecture: Config → Policy → Action.
  3. Allocations, applications, and encashments all post to an immutable Leave Ledger Entry.

Why choose ERPNext + Haritha

  • Open source + full code ownership — no vendor lock-in.
  • Hospital-specific leave patterns (nurse rotation, medical certificates, comp-off from OT).
  • Active community — 5,000+ contributors keep the model fresh.
